Board of Control for Cricket In India

The Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) is the national governing body for cricket in India, headquartered at the Cricket Centre in Mumbai. Founded on December 4, 1928, it is recognized as the wealthiest cricket board globally, with an estimated value of ₹3,308 crore. The organization currently has 30 full-time members representing various state and regional cricket associations across the country.


Yuvraj Singh slams six sixes of Stuart Broad

On September 19, 2007, during the inaugural ICC Men's T20 World Cup, Indian all-rounder Yuvraj Singh made history by hitting six consecutive sixes in a single over. The feat occurred in the Super Eight stage against England at Kingsmead Stadium in Durban, where Yuvraj demolished pacer Stuart Broad for 36 runs.
